Standards

Professional notices

This website is intended to remain within Rule 36 of the Bar Council of India Rules and the accepted distinction between information, education, and solicitation.

Rule 36 and the 2008 schedule

An advocate may furnish website information as prescribed: name; address; telephone numbers and e-mail; enrolment number and date; the State Bar Council of original enrolment and the Council on whose roll the name presently stands; the Bar Association of which the advocate is a member; professional and academic qualifications; and areas of practice. Additional promotional input is avoided.

The chambers page is confined to those heads of information. Superlatives, success stories, client names, case results, rankings, and invitations to “hire” counsel are not used.

Education is kept separate

Law Tie is described as an independent academic initiative. Public legal education, academic discussion of judgments, and syllabus teaching are treated as scholarship and literacy, not as a method of obtaining legal work. The initiative states that it is not affiliated with the Bar Council of India.

Digital manners

In keeping with current professional expectations for digital conduct: no courtroom reels, no use of robes or cause-lists as branding, no AI-fabricated judgments, no comments intended to prejudice a pending proceeding, and no claims of guaranteed bail, stay, acquittal, or examination success.

Intimation

The existence of this website is intended to be intimated to the Bar Council of India in the manner required of advocates who furnish website information. Approval, if required under the applicable schedule, remains the responsibility of the advocate.

Declaration

Adv. Vidushi Soni declares that the professional particulars published here are true to her knowledge, and that educational descriptions are offered without solicitation of legal work.

This page is a statement of intended compliance. It is not a certificate from the Bar Council of India. Rules and circulars may be amended; the site will be revised when particulars or norms change.
