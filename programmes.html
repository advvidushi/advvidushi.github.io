Study desk

Programmes

Short academic modules and personal mentoring for students who wish to read law slowly and correctly.

University

LL.B. academic support

Paper-wise concept classes, bare-act reading, illustrations from leading cases, and revision notes. The aim is understanding, not a shortcut around the prescribed books.

Postgraduate

LL.M. guidance

Help with subject choice, research questions, literature review, and the discipline of academic writing. Supervision of a university dissertation remains with the student’s institution.

Professional enrolment examination

AIBE academic module

Study organised around the subjects notified by the Bar Council of India for the All India Bar Examination, including professional ethics. Law Tie does not conduct the examination and does not claim BCI recognition.

Public service examinations

Judicial-service studies

Academic preparation for state judicial-service papers: constitutional law, procedural codes, evidence, substantive statutes, and the habit of writing a reasoned answer. Current-affairs and judgment-reading sit beside the syllabus, not in place of it.

This is study support. It is not a prediction of selection, and it is not coaching that trades on the dignity of the court.

Personal desk

One-to-one guidance

Limited academic mentoring is offered by appointment: doubt-clearing, study plans, answer review, and reading lists. Sessions remain educational. They are not consultations on a live brief, and they are not a promise of marks.

Manner of study

What a module typically contains

  • Concept explanation with the relevant bare-act language.
  • Selected precedents, placed in their facts and ratio, without sensational comment.
  • Practice questions and, where useful, previous-year academic questions.
  • Doubt support during the agreed academic period.
  • English and Hindi, as the student prefers.

Fees, batch dates, and seat availability for academic modules are confirmed only in direct correspondence. Public posts must not be read as a guaranteed intake or as an official announcement of any examining body.
