The venture

Law Tie

A public classroom for legal study — concepts, bare acts, and case notes — kept accurate, contextual, and non-promotional.

Law Tie was begun in 2021 so that law could be taught in short, careful lessons. The channel and the mentoring desk sit beside the profession; they do not sell court work. Responsible legal education, academic discussion of judgments, and constitutional literacy are the intended use of this space.

Independent of the Bar Council

Law Tie is not affiliated with, recognised by, or endorsed by the Bar Council of India. AIBE modules follow the publicly notified syllabus only. No official seal is claimed.

How the classroom is kept

Accepted academic norms

Accuracy over spectacle

Lessons stay with the statute, the judgment, and the syllabus. Courtroom recordings, robes used as branding, and sensational clips have no place here.

No promised outcomes

No class, note, or conversation promises a pass, a rank, a bail, or a decree. Study is offered. Results remain the student’s and the court’s.

Two languages

Instruction is available in English and Hindi, including recorded and live academic sessions where arranged.

Educational content on this website and on associated social channels is for learning and public legal literacy. It is not a substitute for independent reading of primary sources, and it is not legal advice.
