# Adv. Vidushi Soni · Law Tie

Personal and professional GitHub Pages site: [advvidushi.github.io](https://advvidushi.github.io).

The site has two distinct parts.

1. **Chambers** — informational particulars of Adv. Vidushi Soni, kept within Rule 36 of the Bar Council of India Rules (name, address, telephone, qualifications, areas of practice). Enrolment number and date should be filled from the State roll before public reliance.
2. **Law Tie** — an independent academic classroom (YouTube `@lawtie1`, Instagram `@law.tie`) for LL.B., LL.M., AIBE and judicial-service study, one-to-one academic guidance, and student career counselling. It is not affiliated with the Bar Council of India.

Brand colours: black, ivory, and gold.

## Edit the published particulars

Update `assets/js/config.js` with enrolment number, enrolment date, e-mail, and any change of address or telephone.
