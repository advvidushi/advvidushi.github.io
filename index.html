The venture · Law Tie

A classroom beside the profession

Law Tie began in 2021 as a public legal-studies channel. It now offers short, syllabus-aligned modules, personal academic mentoring, and counselling for those planning a life in law — in college, in academia, or in work.

Academic programmes

Micro-learning, kept within accepted academic norms

Modules are framed as study support. They are not affiliated with the Bar Council of India. They do not claim any official status, ranking, or assured result in an examination or in court.

LL.B.

Concept classes, bare-act reading, and case-law notes aligned to university papers.

LL.M.

Research direction, writing discipline, and subject depth for postgraduate study.

AIBE

Syllabus-oriented preparation for the All India Bar Examination, with ethics treated as a professional duty.

Judiciary

Academic preparation for judicial-service papers: statute, precedent, and answer structure.

For students

Career counselling in law

Guidance is available for school-leavers considering a career in law, and for those already in law college who wish to plan internships, academia, chambers, corporate roles, or judicial-service study. This is educational counselling. It is not legal advice on a personal dispute and not a placement service.
